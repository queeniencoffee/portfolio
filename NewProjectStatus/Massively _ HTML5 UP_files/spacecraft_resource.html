Spacecraft Dynamics
Plant Model in MATLAB & Simulink

The spacecraft plant model is a full 6-Degrees-of-Freedom (6-DoF) rigid-body propagator that integrates the coupled
translational and rotational equations of motion — computing position, velocity, attitude quaternion, and angular
velocity of one or more spacecraft under gravity, drag, third-body, and solar-radiation-pressure accelerations.
