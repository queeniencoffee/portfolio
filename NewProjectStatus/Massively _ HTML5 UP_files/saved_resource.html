Comprehensive Guide: Reduced-Order
Fixed-Wing UAV Guidance Model in MATLAB

In aerospace engineering, a full-fidelity 6-Degrees-of-Freedom (6-DoF) aircraft model requires solving non-linear
Newton-Euler equations of motion using detailed aerodynamic coefficients, control surface deflections, engine thrust
curves, and moment of inertia matrices — a Reduced-Order Model abstracts that into a kinematic point-mass system.
