01   Overview

Physics-Based GEO Electrical Power System Model

This model simulates the electrical power system (EPS) of a ~5.5 kW GEO communications/USSF-class spacecraft across its full 15-year design life: solar-array generation, Li-ion battery cycling and aging, and bus loads across every operational mode. It runs in base MATLAB (R2018b+, no toolboxes required) and completes a 15-year, ~1,361-cycle run in roughly 30–90 seconds.

02   Key Model Equations

Eclipse Geometry, Solar Cell, Battery ECM & Aging

Eclipse duration (cylindrical umbra, circular orbit)

T_ecl = T_orb/180 · acosd[ √(r² − R_E²) / (r·cos β) ]

For GEO this produces a 69.4-minute maximum eclipse, occurring only when |β| < 8.70° — i.e. two ~45-day eclipse seasons per year straddling the equinoxes. Both figures are reproduced by the model and printed as verification checks on every run.

Solar cell (empirical exponential IV fit, "Green" form)

I(V) = Isc·[1 − C1·(exp(V/(C2·Voc)) − 1)], C2 = (Vmp/Voc − 1)/ln(1 − Imp/Isc)

Cheaper than the implicit single-diode equation and accurate through the knee. Under DET/S3R the array is clamped to the bus, so it operates at I(V_bus/Ns) rather than at the maximum power point — the config flag C.sa.regulation switches between 'DET' and 'MPPT' to compare.

Battery (2nd-order Thevenin ECM, per cell, backward Euler)

V = OCV(SOC) − I·R0 − V1 − V2, dSOC/dt = −I/(3600·Q_now)

Sign convention: I > 0 = discharge. R0/R1/R2 carry a mild SOC dependence, an Arrhenius temperature term, and a growth term coupled to accumulated capacity fade.

Aging (semi-empirical, state-shifted)

Q_cal = k_cal·exp(−Ea_cal/RT)·exp(a·SOC) · t_eff^0.75 Q_cyc = k_cyc·exp(−Ea_cyc/RT)·exp(b·C_rate) · Ah_eff^0.55 R0(t) = R0_BOL · (1 + 0.60·fade/fade_EOL)

At each step the effective time/throughput is back-solved from accumulated damage under the current stress, incremented, then recomputed — keeping the fade law monotonic and path-independent under time-varying stress. An independent cross-check runs in parallel: a Wöhler DOD curve N_fail = N0·DOD⁻ᵐ with Miner's rule accumulation.

05   Calibration & Known Limitations

What to Fit Before Trusting This for a Real Trade

k_cal, k_cyc, N0, m, and the OCV table are calibrated placeholders tuned to hit a 20% fade / 80% retention requirement at 15 years — they are not vendor data. The two built-in aging models also disagree by roughly 3×: the semi-empirical law predicts 20% fade, while Miner's rule (N0=1000, m=2) predicts ~7% fade-equivalent damage. That spread is the single largest uncertainty in the battery sizing and should be resolved with cell-level test data rather than averaged.

  • Cylindrical shadow with a linear penumbra ramp; no atmospheric refraction/penumbra photometry.
  • Equatorial orbit assumption (β = solar declination); an inclined or drifting GEO needs a proper sun-vector/orbit-normal dot product.
  • Single lumped battery thermal node held at 20°C — no eclipse-driven thermal swing modeled.
  • Cell-to-cell imbalance, string failure/bypass, and BMS balancing are not modeled.
  • Array degradation uses lumped annual factors rather than SPENVIS/EQFLUX fluence-based remaining-power curves.
